`zapper.mesh` in this directory is a derivative of:

- **"Nintendo Zapper Gun Model"** by **Max Keeley** (Sketchfab user `maxkeeley`)
- Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/nintendo-zapper-gun-model-dd59e968979b46b3a4f8e151c7de37fe
- Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes made (by `scripts/convert-zapper-mesh.py`): triangulated, simplified from about
55 000 to about 6 000 triangles, re-oriented and scaled to metres, smooth normals
recomputed, and the materials baked to vertex colours with the body and grip/barrel
recoloured to the original 1985 grey Zapper (light grey body, dark grey grip and barrel).
Texture coordinates and the original materials are not included.
